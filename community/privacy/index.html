Privacy notice — Sage, the Learn Anything community agent

Effective date: September 17, 2026

Who we are. Learn Anything is a Discord community operated by Nonlinear Labs (Eric Ma and Daniel). The Discord application and bot are operated by Eric Ma on the community’s behalf. The contact for anything in this notice is eric.ma@nonlinearlabs.ai.

What Sage is. Sage is an AI assistant the community runs to help welcome members, answer questions, and keep the server safe. Sage is rolled out in stages: it observes and records server activity, and in channels where we’ve turned answering on, it also answers members who @mention it. This notice explains what Sage records and how that data is handled.

What Sage records

Regardless of mode, Sage records activity metadata: event types (messages, reactions, joins, AutoMod executions), server/channel/message/author IDs, timestamps, reaction emoji, attachment counts, and member details (username, display name, join time, and when Sage last saw you active).

When content capture is on, Sage additionally stores message excerpts capped at 500 characters and text that server AutoMod rules matched. Content capture is off right now — Sage stores metadata only. If we turn it on later, we’ll update this notice and announce it in the server before enabling it.

Sage never stores direct-message content (it doesn’t process DMs at all) and never stores the option values you type into slash commands.

When you @mention Sage a question, Sage keeps a short answer-audit record: who asked, which channel, when, whether it could answer from its community notes, and which safety filters ran. The question’s text itself is stored only if content capture is on — and content capture is off right now.

Why

To run the community agent: onboarding, answering questions grounded in community knowledge, flagging possible rule-breaking for human review, and improving the answers Sage can give. Member history is used to inform human moderation only — never automated punishment.

What Sage doesn’t do

Sage never issues punishments on its own; it flags and logs, and people act. Sage always identifies itself as an AI when it answers you, and Discord labels Sage as a bot. Sage never sends direct messages about moderation decisions; people handle those end to end.

Where it lives

In a database hosted on Modal in the United States, encrypted at rest by Modal’s platform. Only the community operators (Eric Ma and Daniel) have routine access to it. Sage also writes brief operational logs to Modal’s dashboard — IDs, event types, and counts, never message content — for running and troubleshooting the service.

How long we keep it

Activity-log entries are deleted after 90 days (culled daily). Answer-audit records from @mentions follow the same 90-day window. Member records are kept while you’re in the server and until you ask us to delete them. If we add new kinds of records in later phases, we’ll update this notice first.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion at any time: message Eric or Daniel in the server, or email eric.ma@nonlinearlabs.ai. Leaving the server does not automatically delete your activity entries or member record — ask us and we’ll remove them.

Sensitive information

Please don’t post sensitive personal information (health, finances, government IDs) in the server. If a message is flagged, a human reviews it.

Age

Sage operates in our Discord server, and membership follows Discord’s Terms of Service, including Discord’s minimum-age requirements. If you believe we hold data about someone under Discord’s minimum age, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Other services

Discord handles your Discord account and server data under its own privacy policy. When you @mention Sage a question, the question’s text is sent to our AI provider — z.ai, operated by Jingsheng Hengxing Technology Pte. Ltd. — to generate the answer. So Sage can follow the conversation you’re asking in, it also sends a short excerpt of that conversation as context: your recent exchange with Sage and, when you ask inside a reply thread, the thread’s earlier messages (other members’ messages included, shown with their Discord names). Their terms say the content is processed in real time, not saved on their servers, and is not used to train their models; like most providers they keep limited exceptions for abuse monitoring and legal compliance. We recorded those terms before turning question-answering on, and Sage sends all of this text only to produce your answer. Their terms: terms of use (including the API additional terms) and privacy policy (which links their Data Processing Addendum).

If you’re in the EEA or UK

We process community data based on our legitimate interests in operating and keeping safe a community you chose to join. You may ask for access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your data, object to our processing, or ask us to transfer it — using the contact above. You can also complain to your local data-protection supervisory authority (or the UK ICO). We keep your data only as long as described above.

Changes

If we change how Sage handles data, we’ll update this notice and the effective date above.
