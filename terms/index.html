These Deposit Terms govern the deposit stated on your deposit invoice — $100 USD for the standard invitation, or the amount the Organizers arrange with you — that reserves your spot at the Learn Anything Retreat, February 15–19, 2027 (the "Retreat"), run by Non-Linear Labs, LLC and Pandarrow, Inc (the "Organizers"). 1. What the deposit is

  • Travel insurance: Travel insurance, including "Cancel For Any Reason" (CFAR) coverage, is strongly recommended; such coverage generally must be purchased within 14–21 days of your initial payment.

2. Who the reservation belongs to

Deposit reservations are personal to the named participant. Payment by any person on the participant's behalf — an employer, an assistant, a family member — reserves that participant's spot on these terms. The name on the invoice is the participant.

By paying, or having payment made on your behalf, the participant represents that they are at least 18 years of age and legally able to enter a binding contract.

Paying the deposit is your acceptance of these Deposit Terms. It is not acceptance of the Participant Agreement. The Participant Agreement, Liability Waiver & Release, and Privacy Notice are presented for your acceptance before the balance payment, not at the deposit stage.
